lyriic

A free, local-first zen editor for writing poetry and lyrics in meter. Per-line syllable counts, optional meter rulers, and quiet rhyme and synonym helpers — all in your browser. Not a generative AI lyric writer.

Features

  • Per-line syllable counts from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary
  • Optional meter rulers for haiku (5/7/5), iambic pentameter, and common meter
  • Hover or tap a word for rhyme and synonym helpers
  • Drafts stay on your device — no account or cloud sync
  • Works offline after assets load

Tools

  • Syllable counter for poems and lyrics
  • Haiku checker for 5-7-5 syllable patterns
  • Rhyme finder with a local rhyming dictionary
