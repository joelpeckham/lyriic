# Ode to Silence analysis

Ode to Silence — Edna St. Vincent Millay (1921)

## Summary

The speaker searches for Silence as a lost goddess, moving through mythic temples, heaven, and the underworld. Silence promises rest but also resembles oblivion.[1]

```
Grave Silence, lovelier
Than the three loveliest maidens, what of her?
Clio, not you,
```

## Meaning and interpretation

The poem’s crowded names and images enact its paradox: language pursues a condition that language cannot recover. Art approaches silence while continually making sound.[2]

```
Oblivion is the name of this abode: and she is there.
```
