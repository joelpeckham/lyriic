# Pastoral analysis

Pastoral — William Carlos Williams (1917)

This Pastoral analysis explains its meaning, themes, form, and major literary devices.

## Summary

Williams relocates the pastoral from ideal countryside to an urban pavement. Sparrows, a gutter worker, and a minister become subjects for a poem about ordinary value.[1]

```
The little sparrows
hop ingenuously
about the pavement
```

## Meaning and interpretation

The poem reverses social and poetic hierarchy. Useful labor appears more majestic than ceremony, while the ending preserves astonishment instead of a final doctrine.[2]

```
his tread
is more majestic than
that of the Episcopal minister
```
